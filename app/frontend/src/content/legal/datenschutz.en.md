## Hosting and server logs

The website runs on a dedicated server operated for us by Hetzner Online GmbH, Industriestr. 25, 91710 Gunzenhausen, Germany, in a data centre in Germany. Hetzner processes the data as a processor on our instructions.

When you open a page, the server processes technically necessary data: your IP address, the requested address, time, status code, amount of data transferred and the details sent by your browser, such as browser type and referring page. The page cannot be delivered without this data.

- **Access log.** The web server logs every request. The IP address is shortened: to the first 24 bits for IPv4 and to the first 48 bits for IPv6. The logs are deleted after seven days.
- **Application log.** The application logs the method, path, status code and duration of a request, without the IP address and without any form content. It is rotated by size; at most five files of 10 MB each are kept.

We use the logs to keep the service running, find errors and fend off attacks. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a secure and functioning website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## Statistics

**Page views.** To understand the reach of the website and repeated requests, our server counts each request for a public page. No choice on your part is required, and nothing additional is stored in or read from your browser. We count the page request sent anyway. Navigation within the open website is not counted; automated requests are included.

Absolute counts are stored as daily totals. For the deduplicated count, the IP address from the connection already needed to serve the page is normalised and immediately derived using a secret key (HMAC-SHA-256). No plain IP address is stored for these statistics. Each derivation has only its most recent request date, with no history of pages visited, exact times or browser details. Derivations are not linked to accounts, results, messages or consultation requests and are not passed to third parties. Only signed-in editorial accounts see aggregate counts.

The derivation is a personal-data pseudonym, not an anonymous identifier. It and the separately protected key are retained for the lifetime of the statistics dataset so that an IP address returning after a long absence is counted only once in the all-time total. When IP statistics are discontinued or reset, derivations and the key are deleted; backups follow their rotation. We review the need for this retention annually. Daily aggregate counts may remain. Several people can share one IP address, and one person can use different IP addresses; the deduplicated count therefore does not measure unique people.

The legal basis for server-side IP deduplication is our legitimate interest in meaningful reach statistics (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You may object on grounds relating to your particular situation under Art. 21 GDPR. To do so, write to us using the controller's contact details.

**Self-assessment results.** At the end of the AI readiness self-assessment you can choose to tick a box so that your result is stored anonymously. The box is not ticked by default. We use the results to understand what our audience needs and to improve the content. Only if the box is ticked does the page send, when your results are shown:

| Stored | Not stored |
| --- | --- |
| the level you chose for each dimension, the language of the page, the time it was stored | your name, email address, IP address, browser or any other identifier of you |

So that a changed result is updated rather than stored twice, the page creates a random identifier. It is kept only in the memory of this browser window, not in cookies or local storage, and is lost when you reload or close the page. We only store an irreversible hash of it. If you untick the box while the window is open, we delete the result immediately. After that it can no longer be linked to anyone and therefore can no longer be deleted individually. We keep the anonymous results for as long as we need them to assess demand.

The legal basis for sending the result is your consent (§ 25(1) TDDDG). You can withdraw it by unticking the box while the window is open. If the box is not ticked, nothing is sent and you still see your full results.
