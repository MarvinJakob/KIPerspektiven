- **Data:** Number of page views per day. To count repeated views only once, we derive a pseudonym from your IP address and store the day of its most recent view.
- **Purpose:** Understanding the reach of the website.
- **Legal basis:** Our legitimate interest in meaningful reach statistics (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- **Retention:** For as long as we keep the statistics. We review annually whether this is still necessary.
