## Visiting the website

When you visit the website, our server processes your IP address, the requested address, the time, the status code, the amount of data transferred, the browser type and the referring page. This is necessary to deliver the website, find errors and prevent abuse; without this data the page cannot be displayed. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a secure and functioning website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The access log contains only a shortened IP address and goes back at most seven days. When you submit a form, the server remembers your IP address for at most one hour.

## Reach statistics

We count page views per day to understand the reach of the website; we have a legitimate interest in doing so (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). To count repeated views only once, we derive a pseudonym from your IP address and store the day of its most recent view. We store this data for as long as we keep the statistics and review annually whether this is still necessary.

## Contact form

When you write to us using the contact form, we store your name, company, email address, message, the language of the page and the time of receipt to reply to you by email. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); if your message is aimed at a contract with us, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR applies. Providing the details is voluntary, but without a name, email address and message we cannot reply to you. We delete the message no later than six months after receipt.

## Consultation requests

When you request a consultation, we store your email address, the language of the page and the time of the request to contact you by email and arrange an appointment. The legal basis is steps taken at your request prior to a possible contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). The request is voluntary, but without an email address we cannot contact you. We delete it once the appointment has been arranged, and in any case no later than six months after your most recent request.

## Self-assessment results

Only if you tick the box to store your result in the AI readiness self-assessment do we store the level you chose for each dimension, the language of the page, the time and the hash of a random identifier that exists only while the browser window is open. We use this to understand what our audience needs and to improve the content. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG). It is voluntary, and you see your full results without it. If you untick the box while the window is open, you withdraw your consent and we delete your result immediately. After that we can no longer link it to you and store it for as long as we need it for this evaluation.

## Cookies and browser storage

We only store in your browser what is strictly necessary for a function you choose (§ 25(2) TDDDG). This does not require consent. You can switch storage off via "Cookie settings" in the footer.
